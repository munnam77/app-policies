# Napwave privacy policy


Effective 22 November 2026. Applies to the Napwave app for Android (package `app.napwave`).

**Short version:** Napwave has no account, analytics or advertising. It does not collect your data or share it. Your mixes and settings stay on your phone. If you buy Napwave Plus, Google Play handles the purchase and Napwave keeps a small record of it on your phone so Plus keeps working offline. Google's own Play Billing software, which Napwave includes, talks to Google (see Purchases).

## What Napwave stores, and where

Everything Napwave keeps is stored only on your device:

- your saved mixes (their names, the sounds in them and each sound's level),
- your settings (the look you chose, your fade-out length, whether a shared card carries the "Made with Napwave" line, and the last mix you played),
- a small record of your Napwave Plus subscription, if you have one (see Purchases).

There is no Napwave account, no sign-in and no Napwave server. We cannot see, read or recover your data. To remove it, delete a mix in the app, clear Napwave's storage in Android settings, or uninstall Napwave.

## What Napwave collects and shares

Nothing. Napwave has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting service, and Napwave's own code sends no data about you to us or to anyone else. It works fully offline. The only network traffic comes from Google Play and Google's Play Billing library, described under Purchases.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (Android 13 and later): to show the playback controls and the time left in the notification. Napwave asks once, after you first play a sound. Sound plays without it.
- **Foreground service for media playback**: so sound keeps playing with the screen off.
- **Wake lock**: held only while sound is playing, so playback keeps its schedule with the screen off. Released when you pause or stop.

Napwave does not use the microphone, location, contacts, photos, files or camera.

## Backup and moving to a new phone

Napwave does not use Android's automatic cloud backup: your mixes and settings are not uploaded. If you set up a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, your mixes and settings can move across with it, directly between the two phones. That is the only way they leave the phone. Your Plus record is not transferred; a new phone asks Google Play again.

## Sharing

When you share a mix card, Napwave makes an image of the card and a link, and hands them to the Android share sheet; you choose where they go. Napwave does not send them anywhere itself. The card image is written to the app's private cache and is replaced by the next card. The link contains the sounds, their levels and the timer length of the mix, and nothing else.

## Purchases

Napwave Plus is an optional subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment, billing and renewal under its own terms and privacy policy. Napwave never sees your card or payment details. Google Play tells Napwave whether the subscription is active, and Napwave keeps the purchase details Google Play provides (product, purchase token, purchase time and state) in a private file on your phone to keep Plus working offline. Napwave has no server and does not send those details anywhere. The Google Play Billing library that Napwave includes is Google software: it talks to Google Play and, like other Google Play services libraries, may send its own diagnostic and performance information to Google under Google's privacy policy. Napwave does not receive that information and does not control it. This is the reason the app declares the Android network permissions the library brings with it. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play; Napwave's Settings has a shortcut. Clearing Napwave's storage or uninstalling removes the file on your phone; Google keeps its own purchase record under its policy.

## Crash reports

Napwave does not send crash reports. Any crash information stays on your phone. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google in your Android settings, Android and Google Play may collect crash information about apps under Google's own policies; Napwave does not receive or control it.

## Children

Napwave is for adults and is not designed for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Not a medical device

Napwave plays sound. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition and does not track your sleep. If sleep problems continue, see a professional.
