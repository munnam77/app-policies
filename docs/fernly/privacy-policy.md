# Fernly privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026. Applies to the Fernly app for Android (package `app.fernly`).

**Short version:** Fernly has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it collects and shares no data. Your plants, photos and care log stay on your phone. If you buy Fernly Plus, Google Play handles the payment.

## What Fernly stores, and where

Everything you save in Fernly is stored only on your device: your plants (names, species, rooms, photos), their watering and care schedules, your care log and notes, growth photos, and your settings (reminder time, winter rhythm, display). There is no Fernly account, no sign-in and no Fernly server. We cannot see, read or recover your data.

To remove it, delete a plant in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall Fernly.

## What Fernly collects and shares

Nothing. Fernly has no analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking, and nothing you enter in it is sent to us or to anyone else.

## Network use

Fernly's own code does not use the network, and its own manifest asks for no network permission. The only network traffic comes from the Google Play Billing library, which talks to Google Play for Fernly Plus prices and purchases (see Purchases).

## Notifications

Fernly can send one reminder a day, at the time you choose, listing the plants that need care. The reminder is scheduled and built on your phone; its text (plant names) never leaves it. Fernly asks for notification permission only when you choose to turn the reminder on, and it works without it: Today still shows what needs water.

## Photos

Photos come from the Android photo picker or the system camera. Fernly copies the photo you choose into its own private storage. It does not ask for camera, photo library, storage or location permission, and it does not read your other photos.

## Backup and moving to a new phone

Fernly does not use Android's automatic cloud backup: your data is not uploaded. If you set up a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, your plants, care log and photos can move across with it, directly between the two phones. That is the only way your Fernly data leaves the phone.

## Sharing

When you share a plant, Fernly hands its name and photo to the Android share sheet and you choose where it goes. Fernly does not send it anywhere itself. Photos are shared through a temporary read-only link to the app's private storage.

## Purchases

Fernly Plus is an optional subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment, billing and renewal under its own terms and privacy policy. Fernly never sees your card or payment details. The Billing library tells Fernly whether the subscription is active, and Fernly keeps that status on your phone so Plus keeps working offline. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play (Fernly's Settings has a shortcut and the steps). Clearing Fernly's storage or uninstalling removes the local copy; Google manages the Play purchase record.

## Crash reports

Fernly does not send crash reports and has no crash-reporting service of its own. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google in your Android settings, Android and Google Play may collect crash information about apps under Google's own policies; Fernly does not receive or control it.

## Children

Fernly is for adults and is not designed for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
